Why this setting

An online university is the hardest honest test.

No hallway conversations. No registrar's window. Decisions happen inside software, at distance, across time zones, and increasingly with an agent in the loop. The institution still owes every student a reason and every regulator a record.

01

Appeals stop being archaeology

The evidence is already assembled at decision time: the agent, the model, the sources, the confidence, the dissent, the four seats. An appeal becomes a lookup instead of a search through tickets and inboxes.

grade appeal - 2026-AUT
02

Due process is visible, not promised

A detector score is not a finding. The receipt records the vote, the dissent, and the students' disclosure. If the evidence base is thin, the trust score says so instead of hiding behind a confident paragraph.

integrity case - trust 48/100
03

The override is part of the record

When the accessibility office overrules an AI denial, that reversal is not an embarrassment to be scrubbed. It is the proof that human oversight worked, signed by the seats that did it.

accommodation - human override
04

Accreditation becomes a query

Instead of a binder assembled once every seven years, the institution carries a live, hash-linked index of every AI-assisted decision, ready for sampling by an external examiner.
